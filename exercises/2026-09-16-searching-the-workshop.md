# Searching the Workshop

The wooden door is old and heavy, so she heaves it open. A gush of dusty air fills her lungs, and she stands at the entrance coughing as her eyes adjust to the darkness of the empty workshop. She steps in, slightly creeped out by atmosphere - the room dimly lit by a dirty skylight window. It seems larger from the inside than one would estimate, with two long tables laid out with a collection of clamps, stacks of sheet metal, at the back of the workshop shelves with various size drills, welding tools and shields, and other bits and bobs she couldn't identify. Her brows tense as she focusses herself on the task she set out for, and she paces across the workshop, her eyes peeled in the dim light. 'How did it even end up here?', she wonders as her gaze falls upon a stack of strange looking metal cubes, misfit in the environment. 'These don't have any dust on them', she deduces, and begins to move them aside. She gasps with satisfaction at the faint golden glow behind the cubes and she becomes more frantic in her retrieval, reaching out and grabbing the small key. She holds it up in front of her face to admire it, her mind almost maliciously guessing what secrets it might reveal. "What's going on here?", comes a sudden, deep voice from behind. She freezes, a chill runs down her spine. Is this it? Was this all for nothing?
