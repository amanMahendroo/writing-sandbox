# Dialogue Subtext

"Why would you say that?", he exclaims. "Well, it is true isn't it? You know exactly how I like my coffee, yet still put in too much milk. The only possible reason is that you want to annoy me.", she responds. "Of course I don't want to annoy you. I'm sorry. I'll try again next time.". She rolls her eyes and sighs. "What now?", "Nothing. I'm late for work. I think it's just better I make my own coffee from now". "I just want another chance, okay? I promise I can do better. I love you and I just want to make you happy". "I've really heard that enough times", she says as she walks out the door. He hangs his head in defeat. A tear rolls down her eye as she sits in her car.
